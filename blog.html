Quick answers

Asked every week

How long does nano plastia last?

Three to five months on healthy hair, closer to three if you swim or wash daily. It softens gradually rather than dropping out, so there is no hard line where it ends.

Can I colour and smooth in the same visit?

Colour first, then smoothing two weeks later. Doing both in one sitting costs you tone and leaves the hair too porous to hold either result.

What age do you cut children's hair from?

From about eighteen months. The kids' station sits away from the dryers, and a parent stays in the chair with the child throughout.

Do you take walk-ins?

After 5pm on weekdays, subject to a free chair. Everything involving colour, smoothing or skin needs an appointment because of patch testing.
