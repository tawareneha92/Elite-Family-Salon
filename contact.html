Before you book

Booking questions

How far ahead should I book?

Two days for cuts and skin services, a week for colour or smoothing, and six weeks for a bridal date. Saturdays fill first.

Is a patch test needed?

Yes for any colour, smoothing or first-time facial. It takes five minutes and must happen at least 48 hours before the appointment.

Can the whole family come together?

Yes. Tell us how many guests and we block adjacent chairs so nobody waits. The Sunday package covers up to four people.

What if I need to reschedule?

Call or message us up to four hours before with no charge. Bridal dates carry a separate schedule agreed at the trial.

Which payments do you accept?

UPI, all major cards and cash. The quote you agree at consultation is the amount at the till.
