Our story

Built on repeat

Most of our week is booked by people who first came in for a haircut and stayed for everything else. Their children come now too.

That is why the salon is built for families rather than footfall: a kids' station away from the dryers, closed rooms for skin and waxing, and appointment slots long enough that nobody is rushed off the chair.

How a visit runs

Five steps, every time

The same sequence whether you are booked for a trim or a full bridal day.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We look at your hair dry, ask what has been done to it in the last year, and tell you what it can take.

    10 min · no charge
  2. 02

    Written quote

    Service, time and price agreed before anything is mixed. No additions at the till.

    Confirmed in writing
  3. 03

    Prep

    Shampoo, scalp check and a patch test where colour or smoothing is involved.

    Patch test 48 hrs prior
  4. 04

    The service

    One stylist, one guest, one station. Your appointment is never split between two chairs.

    Senior stylist only
  5. 05

    Aftercare

    How to wash it, how long the result holds, and when to come back. Sent to you on WhatsApp.

    Follow-up in 10 days
